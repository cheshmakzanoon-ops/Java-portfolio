# Wire protocol v1

TLS 1.3 wraps a 4-byte big-endian length prefix (maximum body 512 KiB). Body: magic QKV1, version 1, modified-UTF cluster ID, source ID, destination ID, modified-UTF role token, 64-bit correlation, one-byte body tag, typed fields. IDs 0–2 are authenticated peers; 3 is client; 4 is local administration. Frames authenticate before owner dispatch.

Body tags: 1 peer RPC, 2 mutation, 3 GET, 4 administration, 5 response. RPC tags: 1 RequestVote, 2 vote response, 3 AppendEntries, 4 append response, 5 InstallSnapshot chunk, 6 snapshot response, 7 PreVote request, 8 PreVote response, 9 TimeoutNow. Tags 7–9 were added without changing the version-1 framing: an older member rejects them as `RPC_TYPE` and closes the connection, so a cluster must be upgraded together (mixed-version operation is not supported). A PreVote request carries the term its sender *would* campaign in plus its last log coordinates; its response carries the responder's own term and a grant flag. TimeoutNow carries only the sending leader's ID. All RPCs include term and request identity; append includes log coordinates, at most 32 entries, commit and read correlation. InstallSnapshot includes leader, included index/term, offset, bounded total, chunk length and at most 16 KiB of bytes. Chunks activate only when the assembled, checksummed snapshot's cluster and coordinates agree. Interrupted transfers restart at offset zero. Heartbeat retries retain the current chunk correlation until an acknowledgment advances it; repeated last chunks are checked and acknowledged without duplicate assembly. Partial transfers never activate register data.

Mutation: client ID (max 128 chars), positive contiguous sequence, kind PUT/DELETE/CAS, key <=256 bytes, value/expected <=4 KiB each. The digest is SHA-256 of the canonical typed payload, recomputed from validated fields. Results: OK, CAS_MISMATCH with observed value, NOT_LEADER with hint, UNAVAILABLE, UNKNOWN_OUTCOME, RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED, SEQUENCE_GAP, STALE_SEQUENCE, IDENTITY_CONFLICT. A mutation sent to a leader that is handing off leadership gets UNAVAILABLE with the incoming leader as the hint.

Administration (source 4) actions: `status`, `partition`, `heal`, `snapshot`, and `transfer` with the target member as its peer argument. `transfer` is answered only when the hand-off is settled: OK (hint = the target, now leader), `TRANSFER_TIMEOUT` (nothing changed after one second; the old leader keeps serving), `TRANSFER_SUPERSEDED` (a different member became leader), `INVALID_TRANSFER` (target outside 0–2 or the leader itself), `TRANSFER_IN_PROGRESS`, or NOT_LEADER with a hint. Status additionally reports the member's current leader belief (`leader=`). Null and empty byte values differ. There is no JSON or Java serialization fallback.

A connection must complete TLS and send its first authenticated frame within three seconds. Client requests are capped atomically at 256 outstanding calls; reply validation binds source member, destination role and correlation. These admission/lifecycle checks do not change version-1 framing or message fields.
