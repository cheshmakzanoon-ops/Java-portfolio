# Third-party components

Dependencies are resolved from their published artifacts, not copied into source. Maven and Maven Wrapper are Apache-2.0; Spring Boot and supporting Spring libraries are Apache-2.0; JUnit is EPL-2.0; ASM is BSD-3-Clause; Lucene is Apache-2.0; jqwik is EPL-2.0; JaCoCo is EPL-2.0. See published artifact licenses for transitive dependencies. A release SBOM and complete dependency/license audit remain release gates.

All financial, trading and routing fixtures must be synthetic. The master brief is user-provided.
